100% CLEAN award granted by Softpedia

  Feb 13, 2014

AIGraph CAD Viewer has been awarded Softpedia’s 100% CLEAN Award. This verifies that it does not contain any form of malware, including but not limited to: spyware, viruses, trojans and backdoors.

The description text was created by our editors, using sources such as text from CAD Viewer product’s homepage, information from its help system, the PAD file and the editor’s own opinions on the program itself.

AIGraph CAD Viewer has been tested in the Softpedia labs using several industry-leading security solutions and found to be completely clean of adware/spyware components. We are impressed with the quality of your product and encourage you to keep these high standards in the future.

More information about your product’s certification and the award is available on this page, and if you want, you can download AIGraph CAD Viewer from the Softpedia site too.

It’s great that CAD Viewer is recognized as such by an independent party, and such a distinguished party at that. So It’s grateful to the Softpedia team for this award.
